Health Log

A personal health summary tool, used only by its developer.

Health Log reads the developer's own sleep, activity and heart data from Google Health with read-only access, writes a daily summary into the developer's own Google Sheet, and sends a short morning note to the developer's own Telegram chat.

It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up. Only one Google account uses it.

What it does

  • Summarises last night's sleep, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, steps and active minutes.
  • Compares each day with the developer's own recent normal and suggests how hard to train that day.
  • Keeps weekly goals for sleep, steps, active minutes and strength training.

Privacy

Read how the app accesses, uses and stores Google user data in the Privacy Policy.
