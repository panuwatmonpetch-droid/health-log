Privacy Policy

Health Log · last updated 25 November 2026 · Home

Who uses this app

Health Log is a personal tool. It is used only by its developer, with the developer's own Google account. It is not offered to anyone else.

Google user data the app accesses

With the developer's consent, the app uses these read-only Google Health API scopes:

  • googlehealth.sleep.readonly — sleep sessions, sleep stages and measurements taken during sleep
  • googlehealth.activity_and_fitness.readonly — steps, distance, active minutes and exercise
  • googlehealth.health_metrics_and_measurements.readonly — heart rate, heart rate variability, blood oxygen and other body measurements

The app cannot add, change or delete any Google Health data.

How the data is used

The data is used only to build daily and weekly summaries for the developer: sleep, recovery, steps, active minutes and training suggestions. Google user data is not used for advertising and is not used to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models.

Where the data is stored

  • Daily summaries are written to the developer's own Google Sheet.
  • Access tokens are kept in the private settings of the developer's own Google Apps Script project. They are never written to the sheet.
  • The app has no server or database of its own.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold and is not shared with anyone. The only transfer is a short daily summary that the app sends, at the developer's request, to the developer's own private Telegram chat through the Telegram Bot API.

Keeping and deleting data

The data stays in the developer's Google Sheet until the developer deletes it. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; after that the app can no longer read any data.

Limited Use

Health Log's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
